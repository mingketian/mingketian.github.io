StayBERT: Staypoint Detection as the Pretext Task for Trajectory Foundation Models

Riyang Bao*, Mingke Tian*, Andreas Züfle, Liang Zhao

* Equal contribution

Full-size figure ↗

Where people stop matters more than how they move between stops. StayBERT pretrains trajectory foundation models on exactly that: deciding, point by point, whether a person is staying or moving, using both motion and the places around them. A geo-conditioned self-training step then carries the model to new cities without any labels there.

1Problem setup

A trajectory is a sequence of GPS points with timestamps, and every point is either part of a stay or part of a move:

\[\mathcal{T}=\{(\ell_i,t_i)\}_{i=1}^{T},\qquad y_i\in\{\mathrm{M},\mathrm{S}\}\]

where

\(\ell_i\)
the location of point \(i\)
\(t_i\)
its timestamp
\(y_i\)
its label: move (M) or stay (S)

A staypoint \(s\) is a maximal run of consecutive S labels. Trajectories are adaptively resampled (ATR) before training.

2Place view: context attention

Each point is embedded on the sphere and queries three sources of place context:

\[\mathbf{g}_i=\sum_{k}\operatorname{softmax}\!\left(\frac{\mathbf{q}_i^{\top}K_k}{\sqrt{d}}\right)V_k,\qquad k\in\{\text{POI},\ \text{graph},\ \text{region}\}\]

where

\(\mathbf{q}_i\)
a Sphere2Vec embedding of \(\ell_i\), used as the query
\(K_k,V_k\)
keys and values from nearby points of interest, mobility graphs, and region semantics
\(\mathbf{g}_i\in\mathbb{R}^d\)
the place representation of point \(i\)

In parallel, a motion view smooths raw steps with a dilated causal TCN and encodes time gaps and log-speed with a Transformer, giving \(\mathbf{m}_i\in\mathbb{R}^d\).

3Gated motion-place fusion

\[\alpha_i=\sigma\big(W_g[\mathbf{m}_i;\mathbf{g}_i]+b_g\big),\qquad \mathbf{h}_i=\alpha_i\odot\mathbf{m}_i+(1-\alpha_i)\odot\mathbf{g}_i\]

where

\(\alpha_i\)
a per-feature gate between 0 and 1
\([\mathbf{m}_i;\mathbf{g}_i]\)
the concatenated motion and place features
\(\mathbf{h}_i\)
the fused representation of point \(i\)

Where movement alone is ambiguous, such as slow walking near a store, place context can dominate; where place context is sparse, motion takes over.

4Stop and move labeling

\[p(\mathbf{y}\mid\mathbf{h})\;\propto\;\exp\Big(\sum_{i}\psi(y_i,\mathbf{h}_i)+\sum_{i}A_{y_{i-1},y_i}\Big)\]

where

\(\psi(y_i,\mathbf{h}_i)\)
the evidence for label \(y_i\) at point \(i\)
\(A_{y_{i-1},y_i}\)
a learned transition score between consecutive labels

The transition scores make the CRF prefer coherent stay and move spans over flickering labels. Each staypoint is then summarized by pooling its points, weighted by how confidently each is a stop:

5Staypoint embeddings

\[\mathbf{z}_s=\frac{\sum_{i\in s}\pi_i\,\mathbf{h}_i}{\sum_{i\in s}\pi_i},\qquad \pi_i=p(y_i=\mathrm{S}\mid\mathbf{h}),\qquad \mathbf{z}_s\in\mathbb{R}^{128}\]

where

\(\pi_i\)
the CRF marginal probability that point \(i\) is a stop
\(\mathbf{z}_s\)
the embedding of staypoint \(s\)

6Dwell-structured pretraining

\[\mathcal{L}_{\text{dwell}}=\mathcal{L}_{\text{bal-CRF}}+\lambda_{\text{rec}}\,\mathcal{L}_{\text{rec}}+\lambda_{\text{bnd}}\,\mathcal{L}_{\text{bnd}}+\lambda_{\text{seg}}\,\mathcal{L}_{\text{seg}}\]

where

\(\mathcal{L}_{\text{bal-CRF}}\)
a class-balanced CRF loss on whole sequences, the primary term
\(\mathcal{L}_{\text{rec}}\)
point-level coordinate reconstruction
\(\mathcal{L}_{\text{bnd}}\)
transition-level boundary awareness
\(\mathcal{L}_{\text{seg}}\)
segment-level consistency
\(\lambda\)
weights of the auxiliary terms

7Adapting to a new city (Geo-TTST)

In a target city \(\mathcal{D}_{\text{tgt}}=\{(\ell_i,t_i)\}\) there are no labels. The encoder and fusion stay frozen, confident points become pseudo-labels, and only the CRF head is updated:

\[\begin{gathered}c_i=\max_{y}\,p(y_i=y\mid\mathbf{h})\cdot r_{\text{geo}}(\ell_i),\qquad \hat{\mathcal{Y}}=\{(i,\hat y_i):c_i\ge\tau\},\\ \theta_{\text{CRF}}\leftarrow\theta_{\text{CRF}}-\eta\,\nabla_{\theta_{\text{CRF}}}\mathcal{L}_{\text{CE}}(\hat{\mathcal{Y}})\end{gathered}\]

where

\(r_{\text{geo}}(\ell_i)\)
a geographic reliance score for the location
\(c_i\)
the confidence of point \(i\)
\(\tau\)
the pseudo-label threshold
\(\eta\)
the step size

8Conclusion

Using staypoint detection as the pretext task gives trajectory models a representation organized around stays, the units that downstream mobility tasks care about, and geo-conditioned self-training lets that representation move to new cities without labels.
