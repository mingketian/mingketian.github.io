Under review · IEEE Journal of ICHI

Spatiotemporal Evidence Grounding for Vision–Language Parkinson Gait Assessment

Jinrui Fang, Mingke Tian, Zijie Liu, Zihao Zhu, Zhiwen Fan, Song Wang, Ding Ying, Gengwei Zhang, Tianlong Chen

Paper (PDF) ↗Full-size figure ↗

Clinicians rate Parkinson’s gait by watching how a patient walks, and a useful model should explain its score in the same terms. This work scores gait severity from reconstructed 3D motion and grounds every prediction in biomechanical evidence, measured over time, across clips, and from several camera views.

1Problem setup

Walking video is reconstructed into 3D body motion, from which two inputs are derived:

\[\begin{gathered}\mathbf{q}_t=\mathrm{Enc}_{\text{frozen}}(\text{SMPL motion})\in\mathbb{R}^{T'\times 512},\\ \mathbf{c}_t\in\mathbb{R}^{T\times 13},\qquad y\in\{0,1,2,3\}\end{gathered}\]

where

\(\mathbf{c}_t\)
thirteen time-varying biomechanical concepts: pelvis height and tilt; seven foot measures such as clearance, heel-toe distance, and contact; left and right arm swing and flexion; trunk lean and turn angle
\(\mathbf{q}_t\)
motion tokens from a pretrained motion encoder (for example MoMask) that stays frozen
\(y\)
the MDS-UPDRS gait score

2Concept-guided feature adaptation

\[\mathbf{q}^{\text{adapt}}_t=\mathbf{q}_t+\gamma(\mathbf{c}_t)\odot\mathbf{q}_t+\beta(\mathbf{c}_t)\]

where

\(\gamma(\mathbf{c}_t),\ \beta(\mathbf{c}_t)\in\mathbb{R}^{T'\times 512}\)
a per-token scale and shift produced by a conditioning network from the aligned concepts
\(\odot\)
element-wise product

The residual form keeps the pretrained representation unchanged when the concepts add nothing, and moves it toward clinically meaningful directions when they do.

3Evidence pooling across time, clips, and views

\[\mathbf{h}=\mathrm{MaskedPool}\Big(\big\{\mathbf{q}^{\text{adapt}}_{t,c,v}\ :\ t\ \text{valid},\ c\in\text{clips},\ v\in\text{views}\big\}\Big)\in\mathbb{R}^{512}\]

where

\(t,c,v\)
time position, clip, and camera view
\(\mathbf{h}\)
the pooled feature used by both heads

Masking ignores padded or invalid positions, so a patient recorded with fewer or shorter clips is not penalized.

4Two heads, trained jointly

\[\mathcal{L}=\mathcal{L}_{\text{cls}}+\lambda\,\mathcal{L}_{\text{reg}},\qquad \mathcal{L}_{\text{cls}}=-\log p(y^\star\mid\mathbf{h}),\qquad \mathcal{L}_{\text{reg}}=\mathrm{MSE}(\hat{\mathbf{c}},\mathbf{c})\]

where

\(p(y\mid\mathbf{h})\)
the gait-score classifier after a shared projection
\(\hat{\mathbf{c}}\in\mathbb{R}^{13}\)
the predicted concepts from the auxiliary regression head
\(\lambda\)
the weight of the concept term

The concept term keeps the shared representation tied to measurable gait properties, which is what lets each prediction be explained in biomechanical terms.

5Conclusion

Conditioning a frozen motion model on biomechanical concepts, and supervising those concepts jointly, gives gait-severity predictions that are grounded in the same measurements clinicians reason about.
