Trading Portfolio

Prediction-Market Trading

I run an automated strategy on fifteen-minute crypto price contracts (BTC, ETH, XRP, DOGE, BNB), each of which settles on whether the price ends above a threshold. I treat every contract as a short-horizon forecasting problem: statistical models estimate the settlement probability from market data, position sizes follow a risk-capped Kelly rule, and the system quotes and trades continuously. It is built in Python on the exchange API, with all fills and settlements logged for evaluation.
