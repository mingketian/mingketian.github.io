Mingke Tian

Undergraduate Student B.S. Applied Mathematics and Statistics B.S. Computer Science Emory University

I am an undergraduate student in Applied Mathematics and Statistics and Computer Science at Emory University. My research interests lie at the intersection of agentic AI, foundation models, and world models, with the goal of developing learning systems for modeling and decision-making in complex scientific and engineering systems. I am particularly interested in bringing computational and mathematical structure into machine learning, building LLM agents that reason from feedback and experience, and learning world models that capture how states and actions evolve, to support inference, generation, and planning.
