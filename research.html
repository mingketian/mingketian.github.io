Publications

  • CONSTRAINER: Promptable Graph-Structured Optimization via Constraint Conditioning

    Zeeshan Memon, Mingke Tian, Xinyuan Song, Hongwei Jin, Kibaek Kim, Liang Zhao

    NeurIPS 2026, Main Track

    [PDF]

    CONSTRAINER is a constraint-conditioned foundation model for graph-structured optimization. An operator-DAG constraint encoder conditions a heterogeneous graph transformer through cross-attention. It transfers zero-shot to DC optimal power flow and reaches 99.02% feasibility on DCOPF.

  • ATSolver: Agentic Tuning of Sparse Linear Solvers with Numerical Feedback

    Mingke Tian, Yiyun Chen, Ruipeng Li, Tianshi Xu, Yuanzhe Xi

    Under review at ICLR 2027

    [PDF]

    ATSolver is a verifier-grounded LLM agent that tunes sparse linear solvers from numerical feedback. It runs a gated propose, execute, diagnose, and repair loop under resource budgets. On 35 matrices, it beats matched-budget Best-of-6 (75/105 vs. 64/105 accepted) with 30.1% lower solve time.

  • StayBERT: Staypoint Detection as the Pretext Task for Trajectory Foundation Models

    Riyang Bao*, Mingke Tian*, Andreas Züfle, Liang Zhao

    Under review at AAAI 2027

    [PDF]

    StayBERT uses staypoint detection as the pretext task for pretraining trajectory foundation models. A gated motion-place encoder feeds a CRF decoder trained with dwell-structured objectives. Geo-conditioned test-time self-training then adapts the model to new cities.

  • Structured MAGI with Warm Starts for Partially Observed Bayesian ODE Inverse Problems

    Tianshi Xu, Mingke Tian, Yuanzhe Xi

    Under review at IEEE Journal of [journal name] · Emory NSF REU

    [PDF]

    Structured MAGI targets Bayesian ODE inverse problems in which only part of the system is observed. Multi-task Gaussian processes denoise the data, and a physics-informed neural network supplies initial parameter and trajectory estimates. These estimates warm-start MAGI's HMC sampling, which returns estimates with uncertainty quantification.
