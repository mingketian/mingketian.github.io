Prediction Markets Trading

I trade short-horizon crypto price markets, contracts that settle on whether BTC, ETH, XRP, DOGE, or BNB finishes above a price at the end of a fifteen-minute window. I treat each position as a forecasting problem: estimating probabilities from data, sizing risk with a cash-aware Kelly cap, and quoting both sides continuously while inventory and the clock move.
